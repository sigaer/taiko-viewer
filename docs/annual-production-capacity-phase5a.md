# Annual Production Capacity Forecast — Phase 5A

## Decision

**ANNUAL PRODUCTION CAPACITY PHASE 5A — COMPLETE**

The current two-generator service can sustain approximately **102.88 cold jobs/minute** for the observed population mix. A deliberately tail-heavy mix sustained **41.56 jobs/minute**. Repeating the single worst observed task gives a conservative **2.58 jobs/minute** lower bound; the empirical all-`F` model is 11.57 jobs/minute.

The generator and renderer limits remain unchanged:

- `MAX_CONCURRENT_ANNUAL_BUILDS = 2`
- `MAX_CONCURRENT_ANNUAL_RENDERS = 1`
- generator queue cap = 20
- renderer queue cap = 8
- queue TTL = 600 seconds
- algorithm = `annual-observation-v4.5.1`

Phase 4 commit `c6d1cb7260d714191926e1cd7491fa6e52377b9e` (`9.61.13`) was exercised from the canonical tree with the production worker launcher and cgroup properties. It was not deployed. Production summary, job and normalized-cache data were not written or deleted, and concurrency 3 was not tested.

## Method

The inventory was a read-only scan of the live userdata tree on 2026-09-20. Public results contain only aggregates. Private account-level inventory and benchmark evidence are under `/var/tmp/annual-capacity-phase5a` with restrictive permissions.

The 80-account benchmark used six snapshot-count strata and unique accounts: A/B/C/D/E/F = 27/15/10/8/10/10. This preserves a population-weighted core while deliberately oversampling P95/P99/max tails. Source files and canonical song/rating inputs were hardlink-frozen into a private fixture before measurement. Every run used a fresh private normalized cache, private output, concurrency 1, and the production generator bounds (`MemoryHigh=1G`, `MemoryMax=1536M`, `MemorySwapMax=128M`, `CPUQuota=200%`, hard lifetime and `OOMPolicy=stop`). Cache/output directories were removed after metrics and streaming hashes were captured.

The same 80 frozen inputs ran on `17ba810` (`9.61.12`) and `c6d1cb7` (`9.61.13`). All 80 serialized SHA-256 values matched across revisions; every result was schema 4 and `annual-observation-v4.5.1`.

The primary N=2 replay used 60 unique accounts in observed population proportions (A/B/C/D/E/F = 27/14/10/6/2/1). A second replay used all 80 tail-oversampled accounts as a heavy-launch check. Queue simulations use each bucket's empirical Phase 4 service-time distribution, the complete population bucket weights, and a 1.132 calibration factor derived from the real N=2 replay. Reported simulation values are medians of 200 deterministic-seed trials.

Even 98% warm / 2% cold is insufficient for a literal t=0 burst because 40 cold requests exceed the 22-job running-plus-queue envelope. To avoid immediate rejection for 2,000 simultaneous users, cold work must be at most 22 jobs: at least **98.9%** must already be warm, or launch admission must be intentionally spread/retried outside the single burst.

Phase 4 raises effective population-mix throughput by approximately **1.13%**, reduces the modeled 1,500-job drain by 9.9 seconds, and reduces the 2,000-job drain by 13.4 seconds. The modest population-wide uplift is expected: 86% of users have at most 50 snapshots, where fixed interpreter/worker startup dominates. The benefit is concentrated in the heavy tail: bucket F mean fell from 9.708 to 9.165 seconds, and the sampled max fell from 51.348 to 45.931 seconds.

## Recommendation

- Current real-time cold capacity: **102.88 jobs/min** for the population mix.
- Heavy-launch capacity: **41.56 jobs/min** for the deliberately tail-heavy replay.
- Worst-known repeated-task lower bound: **2.58 jobs/min**; empirical all-F model: 11.57 jobs/min.
- `cap=20 / TTL=600s` is acceptable for controlled arrivals up to the modeled 2,000 users over 30 minutes, but unacceptable as the only protection for an uncontrolled simultaneous public launch.
- Before an uncontrolled 2,000-user launch, precompute/cache is required to reach at least **98.9% warm**, or the product must deliberately stagger/retry arrivals. The tested 98% warm case still rejects 18 cold requests.
- Because Phase 4 fingerprints and active normalized-cache coverage are currently zero, existing cache state does not satisfy that launch requirement. Deploying Phase 4 and a separately authorized, disk-gated precompute would be prerequisites; neither action was performed here.
- Keep generator concurrency 2 and renderer concurrency 1. Do not test or enable concurrency 3 as part of this phase.
